# Submitting Your Findings

**Slides and submission: 3:40–4:00 · Demos: 4:15**

Each group has its own folder with two slides already set up: [Community Research folders](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1sQ3vIRFi-q6sm84_0V-1B3Rd6Fwyzl1x?usp=drive_link). Work there during the day. By 4:00, copy your two finished slides into the [shared demo deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1ic9nFhiB6mftG0uKtgwuIJ0GgWwcM-_T/edit?slide=id.p8#slide=id.p8) under the Community Research section, so we can run every demo from one computer.

To copy slides: select them in the left panel, press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on Mac), click into the demo deck, press Ctrl+V, and choose **Keep original styles**.

| Slide | What Goes on It |
|---|---|
| 1. Problem and Solution | A one-sentence headline finding, the problem you found, your strongest evidence, and what you propose. |
| 2. Visuals and Next Steps | One or two visuals, such as a photo of your finding cards, a map screenshot with the confusing part circled, or your block comparison table, plus what should happen next or how to test your finding further. |

Remember the evidence rule: Every finding needs something specific behind it, like a screenshot, a street, a score the map showed, or something a tester said.
