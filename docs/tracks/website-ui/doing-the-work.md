# Doing the work

*From a working change to a submitted one. Submissions close at 4:00; demos start at 4:15.*

## The steps

1. **Work on a branch** of your own fork, starting from `develop`.
2. **Check your work** against your topic's **Done when** list, on your local copy of the site.
3. **Hand it in:** a pull request for code, a shared Drive folder for designs and plans, a video link for recordings.
4. **Fill in the submission form,** one per team, starting your description with your topic number and ending with your AI-use note.
5. **Add two slides** to the shared demo deck and present for two minutes.

## The pages in this section

### [Making website changes](/tracks/website-ui/making-changes)

Recipes for the common changes: adding a page, changing how the map looks, changing the survey, adding an API endpoint or a database column, and loading different data. Also how to check your work and open a pull request against `develop`, and how to troubleshoot.

### [Submitting your work](/tracks/website-ui/submitting-your-work)

The one form every team fills in, question by question; how to share a Drive folder so reviewers can open it; and how to write your AI-use attestation.

Questions at any point? Ask a mentor or moderator. That's what we're here for.
